Privacy Policy

Colab Cola · Effective November 5, 2026

Colab Cola is a locally installed, open-source command-line tool for running Python workloads on Google Colab. This policy explains how the application accesses, uses, stores, and shares information when you authorize Google services. It applies to the Colab Cola CLI and this website.

1. Google information accessed

With your authorization, Colab Cola may access:

  • Google account identity: your account email address and account identifier to verify that Colab and Drive authorizations belong to the same account.
  • Google Drive files: the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope lets Colab Cola create, read, update, and delete specific Drive files used with the application. It does not request general access to every file in your Google Drive.
  • Google Colab: a separate Google Colab authorization is used to request and manage Colab execution sessions and retrieve their status.
  • Cloud project configuration: the onboarding process may inspect or enable necessary project services using permissions you grant through Google Cloud CLI. That is separate from the desktop Drive OAuth grant.

2. Why and how the data is used

Colab Cola uses these permissions only to authenticate your account, stage workloads and their inputs, manage execution, publish or recover checkpoint and progress information, and retrieve verified outputs. Scripts and inputs you choose to submit can be transferred to the configured Google Drive destination and Google Colab runtime; outputs and logs may also be transferred between those services and your computer.

3. Where information is stored

Colab Cola stores configuration, run journals, and some credentials on your device in user-specific locations. Depending on the authorization path, credentials may be stored in protected local files or your operating system's credential store. Files, logs, checkpoints, and recovered outputs may also be stored in the Google Drive folders used for your jobs. Google controls the infrastructure and its own data processing for Google Drive, Colab, and related APIs.

Colab Cola does not operate a central service that requires users to upload their job files or OAuth tokens to the Colab Cola publisher. The CLI's data transfers are designed to occur between your computer and the Google services you authorize.

4. Sharing and disclosure

Information is transmitted to Google services as needed to carry out the actions you request. Colab Cola is not designed to sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or disclose it to unrelated third parties. Data that you include in your workloads may also be processed by dependencies or external services that you explicitly use in those workloads; review those services' policies separately.

5. Retention and deletion

Local Colab Cola configuration, credentials, and journals generally remain on your device until you remove them or use applicable cleanup procedures. Google Drive artifacts remain in your account subject to job-specific cleanup, your own deletion, and Google's retention policies. You can revoke the Colab Cola OAuth grant at Google Account connections. Revoking authorization prevents future authorized access, but does not itself delete existing local files or Drive artifacts.

6. Security

Colab Cola uses browser-based OAuth and PKCE for desktop authorization. It includes safeguards intended to keep credentials out of workload bundles and logs, and uses user-specific credential storage. No security mechanism is infallible. Keep your computer, Google account, and project permissions secure, and do not share authorization codes or tokens.

7. Website data

This website is a static site and does not include application analytics, advertising scripts, or forms. Its hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may process ordinary web-request information under GitHub's privacy statement. External links may take you to sites with their own policies.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as Colab Cola changes, particularly if the application's Google API permissions or data handling change. The current version will be available at this URL. For questions or privacy requests, contact danzel.serrano@gmail.com.
